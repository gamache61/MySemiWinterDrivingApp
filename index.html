Watch the outside temperature. Near 0°C, a road that looks wet may be black ice — especially on bridges.
Your engine brake starts ON. On slippery roads it can lock the drive wheels.
Each gear has a top speed — hold the throttle and the truck holds steady at that speed. Higher gears give less torque and less wheel spin on ice.
